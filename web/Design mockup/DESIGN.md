# Art direction

A contemporary mathematical publication: classical sculptural imagery, editorial serif headlines, precise sans-serif interface text and monospaced mathematical notation. The visual identity is quiet, matte and high-contrast without neon, glow, glassmorphism or gratuitous gradients.

# Design rules

- Exactly four application pages: Home, Audit, Keygen, Toolkit.
- Shared horizontal navigation, footer, typography, spacing and theme switcher.
- Dark and light themes are both first-class themes, not a simple grayscale inversion.
- Burgundy is a restrained accent for actions, active states and selected details.
- Prefer open layouts, thin rules and a small number of purposeful panels over dense card grids.
- Avoid fake publication metadata, fake institutional branding, fabricated metrics and unsupported security claims.
- Desktop-first; mobile layouts stack content naturally and use a collapsible top navigation.
