§ 03 / Exactness Contract

No floating point. No hidden rounding.

Congruence Criterion
a ≡ b (mod m)  ⇔  m | (a − b)

Every operation is evaluated with arbitrary-precision integers. The derivation shows the actual quotients, remainders, residues, and convergents used to reach the result.

A cryptographic calculator is only useful if its arithmetic is inspectable. The toolkit therefore refuses shortcuts that obscure the proof: no floating-point square roots, no probabilistic guesses presented as facts, and no result without its intermediate steps.

Each operation is a small instrument. The left index selects the algebraic question, the center panel fixes the inputs, and the right panel reconstructs the derivation from the same integers the engine used.
